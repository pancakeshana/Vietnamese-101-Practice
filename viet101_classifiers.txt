A LIST OF COMMON VIETNAMESE CLASSIFIERS (Pages 2-3)

1. cái
   - General Meaning: inanimate thing, piece of furniture
   - Examples: cái bàn (table), cái ghế (chair), cái tủ lạnh (fridge)

2. con
   - General Meaning: animal
   - Examples: con chó (dog), con mèo (cat), con bò (cow)

3. cây
   - General Meaning: tree
   - Examples: cây táo (apple tree), cây chanh (lemon tree)

4. chiếc
   - General Meaning: a separate or moving thing
   - Examples: chiếc xe (vehicle), chiếc máy bay (plane)

5. chai
   - General Meaning: bottle
   - Examples: chai bia (bottle of beer), chai nước (bottle of water)

6. chén/tô
   - General Meaning: small/large bowl
   - Examples: chén cơm (bowl of rice), tô phở (bowl of "pho")

7. cuốn/quyển
   - General Meaning: something like a book
   - Examples: cuốn sách (book), cuốn vở (notebook), cuốn lịch (calendar), cuốn từ điển (dictionary)

8. đôi
   - General Meaning: a pair (of shoes, chopsticks)
   - Examples: đôi giày (pair of shoes), đôi đũa (pair of chopsticks)

9. ly
   - General Meaning: glass/cup
   - Examples: ly rượu (glass of wine), ly bia (glass of beer)

10. ngôi
   - General Meaning: house, temple, star
   - Examples: ngôi nhà (house), ngôi chùa (temple), ngôi sao (star)

11. người/ông/bà/anh/cô
   - General Meaning: people
   - Examples: ông bác sĩ (doctor), anh sinh viên (male student)

12. trái/quả
   - General Meaning: fruit
   - Examples: trái táo (apple), trái cam (orange), quả xoài (mango)

13. tờ
   - General Meaning: paper, newspaper, bill
   - Examples: tờ giấy (sheet of paper), tờ báo (newspaper), tờ 100 đô (100-USD bill)

14. thùng/hộp
   - General Meaning: box
   - Examples: thùng bia (carton of beer), hộp quà (box of presents/gifts)
